**Final Rule (October 30 2026)**  
**Accreditation, Innovation, and Modernization: The Secretary’s Recognition of Accrediting Agencies; Institutional Eligibility Under the Higher Education Act of 1965, as Amended; Student Assistance General Provisions** – The Department is updating the rules that recognize accrediting agencies and set eligibility for higher‑education institutions under the Higher Education Act. The changes implement Executive Order 14279, bring the regulations into tighter alignment with the law, and aim to lower the regulatory burden on schools and accrediting bodies. This affects colleges, universities, and the accrediting agencies that evaluate them, as well as students who receive federal aid.  
[Read the rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/30/2026-22174/accreditation-innovation-and-modernization-the-secretarys-recognition-of-accrediting-agencies)

**Proposed Rule (November 5 2026)**  
**Proposed Priorities and Definitions – Secretary’s Supplemental Priorities and Definitions** – The Secretary is proposing amendments to several existing “Supplemental Priorities and Definitions” notices covering evidence‑based literacy, education choice, meaningful learning opportunities, career pathways, workforce readiness, and artificial‑intelligence‑in‑education programs. The goal is to ensure the Department’s grant‑making priorities reflect the full range of learners, incorporate expertise from partner federal agencies, and apply lessons learned from FY 2026 grant competitions. The proposal would affect any organization that applies for or receives federal education funding under those priority areas.  
[Read the proposal](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/05/2026-22615/proposed-priorities-and-definitions-secretarys-supplemental-priorities-and-definitions)
